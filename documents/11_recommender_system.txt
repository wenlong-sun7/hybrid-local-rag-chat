A recommendation system is an artificial intelligence and machine learning tool that predicts what a user might like or want to buy based on past behavior, preferences, and data patterns. It helps people find relevant movies, products, or songs without searching manually.Main Types of Recommendation SystemsCollaborative Filtering: Looks at what similar users like to guess what you will enjoy.Content-Based Filtering: Suggests items that share features or traits with things you liked before.Hybrid Systems: Mixes both collaborative and content methods to give better and more accurate picks.Common Real-World UsesStreaming apps like Netflix or YouTube recommend shows and videos.Online stores like Amazon suggest products you might want to buy.Music platforms like Spotify build custom daily playlists.Social media sites use them to feed you posts and ads.
